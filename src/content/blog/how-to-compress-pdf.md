PDF files can get massive, especially when they contain high-resolution images or scanned documents. This makes them difficult to email, upload, or store efficiently. 

While there are many online PDF compressors, almost all of them require you to upload your sensitive documents to their servers. This can be a huge privacy risk for confidential contracts, medical records, or personal data.

## The Privacy-First Approach

At **ToolsMani**, we built our [PDF Compressor](/tools/pdf-compress) completely differently. 

Instead of sending your file to our server, we send the *compression software* to your browser. Your computer's processor does all the work locally.

### How to use it:

1. Open the [ToolsMani PDF Compress](/tools/pdf-compress) tool.
2. Select or drag and drop your PDF file.
3. Wait a few seconds for your browser to optimize the data structure and remove unused elements.
4. Click **Download**.

Your file size is reduced, and your document never left your device. 

### Why is my PDF not getting smaller?

Sometimes, a PDF is already as compressed as it can be. If a PDF is entirely made of raw text, there isn't much to compress. Compression mostly works by reducing image DPI and removing redundant object streams within the PDF structure.
